How to Use

Using These Checklists

These checklists are designed to be printed or viewed on your phone while setting up. Each item is a step you can check off as you complete it.

  • On your phone: Open the guide link, scroll to the checklist section, and use it as a reference while you set up your device or router.
  • To print: Open the guide on a desktop browser, scroll to the checklist section, and use your browser's print function. Print to paper or save as PDF.
  • For a wall or counter: Print in A4 and pin it near the equipment you are setting up. Useful for café operators and office managers.
  • Data usage: Loading a guide checklist uses minimal data. These pages are designed to work on mobile data connections without video or heavy images.

Each checklist is part of a full guide that explains the reasoning behind every step. If you are unsure what a checklist item means, read the full guide for that section.
