## 11. When to Call a Technician

Call a technician if:

- You cannot access the router or admin account.
- You are changing settings for a business, school, church, or café with many users.
- The device affects payments, security cameras, business email, or customer internet.
- You suspect hacking, malware, or account compromise.
- You are unsure and cannot safely undo the change.
