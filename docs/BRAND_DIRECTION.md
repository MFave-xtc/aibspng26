# AIBS Digital Setup Guides — Brand Direction

**Version:** v1.0  
**Status:** DRAFT — Ready for design implementation  

---

## 1. Brand Identity

**Brand Name:** AIBS Digital Setup Guides  
**Parent Brand:** AI Digital Bridge Solutions PNG  
**Core Theme:** Built for PNG Digital Trust  
**Primary Message:** Free practical digital setup guides built for Papua New Guinea.

---

## 2. Visual Direction

The website should use the official AIBS visual direction provided by the owner:

- Dark navy / black background.
- Gold highlights.
- Red warning/accent tones.
- Gold Bird of Paradise.
- Gold outline map of Papua New Guinea.
- Digital bridge / network line styling.
- Premium, trusted, PNG-grounded advisory tone.

---

## 3. Logo Usage

Use the provided AIBS logo as the official site identity.

Recommended placements:

1. Header left.
2. Homepage hero.
3. Footer.
4. Checklist cover pages.
5. Social media post templates.
6. About AIBS page.

Do not distort, recolour, crop aggressively, or place the logo over busy backgrounds without contrast protection.

---

## 4. Cultural Identity Elements

### Gold Bird of Paradise

Use as:

- Hero accent.
- Page banner detail.
- Footer watermark.
- Checklist cover icon.
- Social media launch graphic.

Do not overuse inside every guide article.

### Gold PNG Map Outline

Use as:

- Homepage background accent.
- Section divider.
- Footer watermark.
- About page cultural anchor.
- Category banner accent.

The map should be subtle and not reduce text readability.

---

## 6. Typography Direction

Use a clean, readable, professional font stack.

Recommended:

```css
font-family: Inter, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Arial, sans-serif;
```

For large hero headings, a stronger display font may be used, but body text must remain highly readable on mobile.

---

## 7. Visual Mood

The website must feel:

- PNG-grounded.
- Trustworthy.
- Practical.
- Premium but accessible.
- Safety-first.
- Clear for non-technical users.
- Not academic.
- Not childish.
- Not generic tech-blog style.

---

## 8. Homepage Hero Style

Recommended hero layout:

- Dark navy background.
- AIBS logo in header.
- Gold PNG map outline in background.
- Gold Bird of Paradise near the map.
- Main headline in white with gold emphasis.
- Gold divider line.
- Two call-to-action buttons.

Hero headline:

> Free Digital Setup Guides Built for Papua New Guinea

Hero subheadline:

> Step-by-step ICT, internet safety, cybersecurity, small office, internet café, and gaming zone setup guides for PNG homes and SMEs.

Buttons:

- Start with Internet Safety
- Browse All Guides

---

## 9. Guide Card Design

Each guide card should include:

- Category label.
- Gold icon.
- Guide title.
- One-sentence benefit.
- Difficulty level.
- Estimated time to complete.
- Checklist availability badge.

Example card metadata:

```yaml
category: Internet Safety
difficulty: Beginner
time_required: 20 minutes
checklist: Yes
png_context: High
```

---

## 10. Tone of Voice

Use direct, practical PNG-grounded language.

Good:

> Before you change your router settings, take a photo of the current setup and write down the Wi-Fi name and password. This helps you recover quickly if something goes wrong.

Avoid:

> Leverage best-in-class cyber hygiene practices to optimise household digital resilience.

---

## 11. Brand Phrases

Approved phrases:

- Built for PNG.
- Practical steps. Safer internet.
- Secure by default.
- Governed digital setup.
- Step-by-step guidance for PNG homes and SMEs.
- Technology advice that fits PNG conditions.
- Free guides. Practical checklists. Safer digital habits.

---

## 12. Visual Restraint Rules

Do not:

- Overuse glow effects.
- Make text too small.
- Put long paragraphs over textured backgrounds.
- Use too many icons in one section.
- Make the website look like a gaming-only brand.
- Overuse red except for warnings and risk notices.
- Use stock photos that do not fit PNG context.
