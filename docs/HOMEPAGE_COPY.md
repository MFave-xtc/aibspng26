## PNG Reality Check Section

# Built Around PNG Conditions

Generic overseas advice often assumes reliable electricity, cheap unlimited internet, easy replacement parts, and high digital literacy.

PNG users need advice that accounts for:

- Power interruptions.
- Mobile data costs.
- Shared devices.
- Second-hand equipment.
- Variable internet speeds.
- Informal buying and selling.
- Limited technical support.
- Child and youth online safety.
- Cyber scams and fake links.
- Small business budget constraints.
