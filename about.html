Why This Exists

Why This Guide Hub Exists

Most digital setup guides are written for users in high-income countries with fast unlimited internet, reliable electricity, easy access to replacement parts, and readily available technical support. That does not describe most of Papua New Guinea.

This guide hub exists to make safe, useful digital setup knowledge available to more Papua New Guineans. The focus is practical: clear steps, PNG context, security warnings, checklists, and guidance that ordinary users can follow without needing to hire a technical expert for every task.

Every guide in this hub is written around real PNG conditions — variable internet quality, mobile-first access, power interruptions, shared devices, second-hand equipment, and different levels of digital literacy across provinces and demographics.

Local Context

Technology Advice Must Fit PNG Conditions

Generic technology advice from overseas often fails PNG users because it assumes conditions that do not exist in much of Papua New Guinea. AIBS Digital Setup Guides is built specifically around the following local realities:

PNG Conditions These Guides Account For
  • Frequent power outages — Guides include UPS and surge protection advice, and are designed so you can stop and resume safely during an outage.
  • High mobile data costs — Steps note data-heavy actions and offer alternatives where possible. Mobile-first users are accounted for throughout.
  • Shared devices in homes and offices — Guidance covers multi-user setups rather than assuming each person has a personal device.
  • Second-hand and refurbished equipment — Buying checklists and factory-reset instructions are included for used hardware.
  • Limited local technical support — Guides are written so ordinary users can follow them. Clear warnings indicate when professional help is genuinely needed.
  • Variable internet speeds — Guides account for slow and inconsistent connections from PNG ISPs, especially outside NCD.
  • PNG-specific scams and fraud — Cybersecurity content references scam patterns and fraud tactics known to target PNG users.
