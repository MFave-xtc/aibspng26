About PISC

A trusted ICT & security partner, built on authorized partnerships.

In Papua New Guinea, PISC is a Master Distributor for Ruijie Networks, NearHub Video Conferencing Solutions, Dahua Technology, and DJI Enterprise Drones.

We are also an Authorized Regional Distributor and Solutions Partner for a wide range of globally recognized technology brands — spanning networking, access control, IT infrastructure, enterprise software, and video surveillance.

Through these strategic partnerships, we deliver comprehensive, enterprise-grade ICT, security, and integrated technology solutions to government agencies, commercial enterprises, and infrastructure projects across Papua New Guinea.
