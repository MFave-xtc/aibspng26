Privacy

How AIBS Digital Setup Guides handles information submitted through this website.

What Data Is Collected

This website is a static public guide hub. In normal browsing, no personal data is collected by the site itself.

The only route by which personal data may be submitted is through the contact form. The contact form can collect:

  • Name — Your full name as entered in the form.
  • Organisation — Your business, school, church, or organisation name (optional).
  • Province / Location — Your selected province in Papua New Guinea.
  • Contact information — Your email address or phone number.
  • Request details — A description of the setup help you are requesting.

How the Contact Form Works

The contact form on this website uses a mailto fallback. When you submit the form, it opens your device's email application with your request details pre-filled in a new email. You then send the email from your own email account.

No data is stored on this website's server. The website does not have a backend server that stores form submissions. Data is only transmitted when you choose to send the email from your own email application.

Because the contact form uses your email client, your submission is subject to the privacy policy of your email provider (e.g., Gmail, Digicel email, or any other provider you use).

How Submitted Data Is Used

Information submitted through the contact form is used only to respond to your help request. AIBS does not use submitted contact information for marketing, mailing lists, or third-party sharing. Submitted information is handled per AIBS internal policy and is retained only as long as necessary to address your request.
