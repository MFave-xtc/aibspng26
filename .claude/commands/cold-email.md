## Core principles

- Sound like **a smart colleague who noticed something relevant** — not a salesperson
- Subject line: lowercase, 2–5 words, boring but curiosity-inducing
- Open with a specific observation or hook, NEVER "I hope this email finds you well"
- 100–150 words maximum (excluding subject and signature)
- One clear, low-friction ask only
- No buzzwords: synergy, leverage, circle back, per my last email

## Before I write, I need

1. **Recipient** — name, role, organisation
2. **Service to pitch** — which AIBS service?
3. **Hook / context** — what triggered this email? (news, tender, referral, cold)
4. **Goal** — book call, send proposal, or intro only?
