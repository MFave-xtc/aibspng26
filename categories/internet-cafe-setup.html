Internet Café Setup

For new and existing internet café operators in PNG — practical guides covering startup planning, hardware selection, network design, and customer safety.

PNG Reality Check

Internet cafés in PNG serve a critical role providing internet access, printing, scanning, and document services. Success depends on location (near schools and government offices), reliable power supply, appropriate hardware for budget, and clear pricing and policies. Competition from mobile internet is increasing but cafés remain essential for document preparation and printing services.
