Home Internet Safety

Practical guides for PNG households — secure your router, protect your passwords, recognise scams, and keep your devices and personal information safe.

PNG Reality Check

Most PNG homes access the internet through a single Wi-Fi router connected to an ISP. Default router settings and weak passwords are the most common risks. Mobile-first households often have multiple phones sharing one router. Second-hand devices may have pre-existing security problems.
