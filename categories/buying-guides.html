PNG Reality Check

Buying ICT equipment in PNG requires checking for local warranty support, availability of replacement parts, and compatibility with PNG power supply (240V, 50Hz). Second-hand equipment from informal markets carries risks including unknown history, missing parts, and no warranty. These guides help you ask the right questions before purchasing.
