Small Office Setup

Guides for SME owners, office managers, church administrators, and school staff — set up reliable internet, configure your network, and protect your office from common digital risks.

PNG Reality Check

Small offices in PNG typically run a mix of Windows PCs, Android phones, and shared printers on a single router. Power interruptions are common — UPS protection for networking equipment is important. Most SMEs do not have dedicated IT staff, so these guides are designed for owner-operators and non-technical managers.
