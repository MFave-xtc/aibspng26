Children & Family Safety

For parents and guardians in PNG — practical guides to set up safer internet for children, filter harmful content, manage screen time, and agree on family internet rules.

PNG Reality Check

Children in PNG are increasingly using smartphones, often through shared family devices. Many children are introduced to social media at a young age. Internet cafés and school computers also provide access. Parents who take practical setup steps — even basic ones — significantly reduce their child's exposure to unsafe content and online contact risks.
