Essential guides for everyone in PNG — protect your passwords, turn on two-factor authentication, spot scams, and keep your personal information safe online.

PNG Reality Check

Cybercrime targeting PNG users includes account takeovers (especially Facebook), marketplace fraud, fake job offers, and phishing messages impersonating banks and telecoms companies. Most incidents can be prevented with basic security measures covered in these guides.
