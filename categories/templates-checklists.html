Free printable ICT setup checklists and planning tools for PNG homes, offices, internet cafés, and gaming zones — designed to work when printed in black and white on standard paper.

PNG Reality Check

Printable checklists help users who work through setup tasks without constant internet access, share setup instructions with staff, or document their setup for future reference. These are designed to work when printed in black and white on standard paper.

How to Print These Checklists

Open the guide page linked below and scroll to the checklist section. Use Ctrl+P (Windows) or Cmd+P (Mac) to open your browser print dialog, or tap the browser menu and choose Print. Checklists are styled to print cleanly in black and white on A4 paper.
