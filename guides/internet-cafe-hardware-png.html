Internet Café Hardware Requirements: Budget, Standard, and Premium Setups

Audience: Internet café entrepreneurs, ICT helpers, planning new or upgrading existing cafés
Difficulty: Intermediate
Time: 30 minutes
Last reviewed: June 2026

1 Quick Summary

This guide provides practical hardware checklists for three internet café setup tiers: Budget (3–5 computers), Standard (6–10 computers), and Premium (11–20 computers). It covers computers, networking, power protection, printing, cabling, and ongoing maintenance requirements.

All prices in this guide are indicative planning estimates only. They require confirmation with current PNG hardware suppliers before being used in a real budget. Hardware prices change regularly, and import availability in PNG means some items may need to be ordered specifically.

All Prices Are Estimates — Verify Before Purchasing

Do not purchase hardware based on the estimates in this guide without first getting current quotes from PNG suppliers. All price ranges are marked as requiring confirmation. Use this guide to understand what to buy and what specifications to ask for — then get real quotes.

2 PNG Reality Check

PNG Reality Check

Hardware availability in PNG is primarily through Port Moresby distributors, with a smaller number of suppliers in Lae, Madang, and other provincial capitals. New hardware from verified distributors comes with warranty support, but warranty claims outside Port Moresby can be difficult — factor in transport costs if you are in a regional location.

Second-hand hardware is widely available in PNG, particularly computers. Second-hand computers can reduce startup costs significantly but carry risk — older hardware is more likely to fail, may not be eligible for security updates, and comes with no warranty. Test second-hand hardware thoroughly before purchasing. ASSUMED — second-hand market prevalence in PNG ICT retail; confirm with market survey

Power protection is not optional in PNG. UPS units protect computers from sudden power cuts that can cause data loss and hardware damage. A generator is necessary for cafés in areas with frequent or extended outages — factor both UPS and generator costs into your planning from the start.

Physical security is a higher concern in PNG than in many other countries. Hardware theft is a real risk in some areas. Budget for security measures — locks, grilles, alarm, and CCTV where possible — as part of the hardware plan, not as an afterthought.

3 Hardware Buying Tips for PNG

  • Check warranty duration and service location: Confirm whether warranty is serviced locally or requires shipping to Port Moresby or overseas. A 1-year on-site warranty is significantly more useful than a 2-year return-to-base warranty if you are in a regional location.
  • Ask for an installation demonstration: For routers, switches, and printers, ask the supplier to demonstrate the equipment working before you take delivery. This is your opportunity to identify defective units before paying.
  • Compare at least 2–3 suppliers for major items: Computer and networking hardware prices vary significantly between PNG suppliers. Getting multiple quotes is worth the time for large purchases.
  • Request itemised quotes in writing: Verbal quotes are unreliable. Get written quotes that specify the exact model, specifications, quantity, warranty period, and total price including any delivery cost.
  • Factor in import duty: Hardware imported into PNG may attract customs duty. REQUIRES USER CONFIRMATION — current PNG customs duty rates on computers, networking equipment, and UPS units

4 What Not to Buy

Avoid Why Better Alternative
Computers older than 5 years without testing Older computers may not meet Windows 11 requirements, are more prone to failure, and may no longer receive security updates — creating customer data risk and maintenance burden Test any second-hand computer for hardware faults, confirm Windows 11 compatibility, and verify remaining support lifetime before purchasing
No-name or unbranded UPS units without a test certificate Cheap UPS units may fail to protect against power spikes, deliver false protection readings, or have inadequate battery backup time — discovered only during an actual power event Use established UPS brands: APC, Eaton, CyberPower. Request a load test from the supplier before accepting delivery
Second-hand printers without testing all functions Printer problems — blocked nozzles, paper feed failures, missing cartridges — are not visible without a test print. Second-hand printers may have hidden fault conditions that only appear under regular use Test print at least 10 pages, including a double-sided page if the printer claims duplex support, before purchasing
Single-band (2.4GHz only) Wi-Fi routers for customer use 2.4GHz only routers cannot handle multiple simultaneous users without significant performance degradation. In a café environment with 5+ connected devices, the connection quality will be poor Use a dual-band router (2.4GHz + 5GHz). Business-grade routers designed for multi-user environments are preferred over home routers
The cheapest available option without checking reliability reviews In a café, equipment failure directly translates to lost revenue and unhappy customers. A computer that fails after 3 months costs more than the saving at purchase Prioritise known brands with verified local warranty support. Reliability over time costs less than cheap hardware that fails

5 Budget Tier — 3 to 5 Computers

Estimated total range: K8,000 – K18,000 REQUIRES USER CONFIRMATION — verify with current PNG supplier quotes

Suitable for: a first café, community access point, or a small supplementary income operation. This tier uses entry-level but capable hardware. Performance will support browsing, document work, email, and social media — not gaming or video editing.

Component Budget Tier Specification Notes
Computer (per unit) Intel Core i3 10th gen or AMD Ryzen 3; 8GB RAM; 256GB SSD; Windows 11 Minimum spec for acceptable Windows 11 performance. Confirm Windows 11 hardware requirements before purchase — TPM 2.0 required
Monitor (per unit) 19–21 inch LED, 1080p resolution preferred, 60Hz 1080p preferred over 720p — better readability for document work and government portals
Router Budget dual-band router — TP-Link Archer series or similar Must be dual-band (2.4GHz + 5GHz). Check simultaneous connection limit — minimum 15 for 5 computers plus phones
Network switch Unmanaged 8-port switch (TP-Link or D-Link) Provides wired connections for all computers — more reliable than Wi-Fi for desktop computers
UPS (per computer) 600VA UPS — protects against short outages and power spikes Provides 5–15 minutes of backup power per computer — enough to save work and shut down safely. APC Back-UPS or equivalent
Printer Basic inkjet printer — Epson EcoTank or HP equivalent EcoTank models have lower per-page running cost than cartridge inkjets. Check ink availability from local suppliers
Network cabling Cat5e or Cat6 ethernet cable from switch to each computer Pre-made patch cables or have cabling terminated on-site. Cat6 preferred for longevity but Cat5e is sufficient for up to 1Gbps
Furniture Basic computer desks and chairs — one per computer position Desks should have cable management to keep wiring tidy and reduce trip hazards

6 Standard Tier — 6 to 10 Computers

Estimated total range: K25,000 – K50,000 REQUIRES USER CONFIRMATION — verify with current PNG supplier quotes

Suitable for: a full-service community café or a viable small business operation with printing, scanning, and document services. This tier uses more capable hardware with better networking equipment that handles multiple simultaneous users reliably.

Component Standard Tier Specification Notes
Computer (per unit) Intel Core i5 or AMD Ryzen 5; 16GB RAM; 512GB SSD; Windows 11 16GB RAM gives noticeably better multitasking performance. 512GB SSD provides storage for Windows updates, user files, and applications without running low
Monitor (per unit) 22–24 inch LED, 1080p, 60Hz minimum Larger screens improve usability for document work and reduce eye strain for customers working longer sessions
Router Business-grade dual-band router — Mikrotik hAP ax2/ax3 or TP-Link EAP series (access point + separate router) Business routers provide better simultaneous user handling, QoS (traffic prioritisation), and VLAN support for separating customer and staff networks
Network switch Managed 16-port switch — TP-Link TL-SG116E or similar Managed switch allows VLAN configuration and port monitoring — useful for separating customer and staff traffic and diagnosing issues
UPS — computers 1000VA UPS per group of 3 computers Higher capacity UPS covers more computers per unit. Provides 10–20 minutes of backup depending on load
Backup power Small generator — 3.5 to 5 kVA petrol or diesel For extended outages. REQUIRES USER CONFIRMATION — PNG generator pricing, fuel availability, and generator sizing for 6–10 computers
Printer Laser printer — Brother or HP LaserJet Laser printing is significantly more cost-effective per page than inkjet for high-volume café use. Check toner cartridge availability locally
Scanner Flatbed scanner or all-in-one printer/scanner Scanning to email or USB is a valued service. Confirm USB flash drive compatibility — customers will bring their own drives
NAS (optional) 2-bay NAS for local file storage or print server Allows centralised storage for shared files or print queue management. Not essential at this tier but useful for larger operations
Network cabling Cat6 ethernet throughout — terminate at a wall patch panel if premises allow A patch panel makes future reconfiguration and troubleshooting easier. Label all cables clearly

7 Premium Tier — 11 to 20 Computers

Estimated total range: K60,000 – K120,000+ REQUIRES USER CONFIRMATION — verify with current PNG supplier quotes and ISP pricing for multi-connection setups

Suitable for: a serious commercial café operation, a gaming zone, or a business centre in a high-traffic location. This tier requires more substantial investment, enterprise-grade networking, and active management. It should not be considered without verifiable demand and revenue projections.

Component Premium Tier Specification Notes
Computers (customer) Intel Core i5 or i7; 16GB RAM; 512GB SSD; Windows 11; dedicated GPU for gaming tier A dedicated GPU (graphics card) is required if offering gaming services. Confirm gaming titles' specific hardware requirements before purchasing
Monitors (customer) 24 inch, 1080p, 144Hz for gaming; 24 inch 60Hz for standard stations High refresh rate monitors are expected by gaming customers. Standard browsing stations do not need 144Hz
Router / Firewall Mikrotik RB4011 or equivalent enterprise router with firewall capability Enterprise router with full VLAN support, QoS, bandwidth management, and logging. Mikrotik provides strong capability at competitive cost
Network switch Managed switch with VLAN support — 24 or 48 port Supports complete separation of customer, gaming, staff, and management networks via VLANs
Internet connectivity Primary ISP connection + backup ISP connection with automatic failover Dual ISP setup ensures continuity if one provider goes down. Mikrotik supports failover configuration natively
UPS — network equipment Rack-mount UPS for server and network equipment (router, switch, modem) Network equipment must stay running even when computers are shut down during outages — separate UPS for infrastructure
UPS — customer stations Individual 600–1000VA UPS per station or per group of 2–3 Gaming computers draw more power than standard browsing computers — confirm UPS load capacity against actual computer power draw
Generator Automatic transfer switch (ATS) generator — sized for full café load ATS generator switches automatically on power failure without manual intervention. REQUIRES USER CONFIRMATION — ATS generator pricing and availability in PNG
CCTV 4–8 camera IP CCTV system covering all customer areas and entry/exit points Deters theft and provides evidence in case of incidents. Network IP cameras can be monitored remotely
Access control Keypad or electronic lock for back office and server/network area Prevents customer access to network equipment and staff-only areas
Printer High-volume laser printer or multifunction device (print, scan, copy) Higher page-per-minute rating to handle peak demand. Consider a second printer as backup at this scale

8 Regular Maintenance Schedule

Hardware lasts longer and performs better with regular maintenance. A café that neglects maintenance will see increasing hardware failures, poor customer experience, and higher replacement costs over time.

Frequency Task Applies To
Daily Clear browser history and session data on all customer computers; restart computers; verify printer has adequate ink or toner All tiers
Weekly Run a full malware scan on all customer computers; check router logs for unusual traffic; verify backup ISP connection (Premium tier) All tiers
Monthly Apply Windows and browser security updates (schedule during closed hours); clean dust from computer vents and fans; inspect UPS status indicators; check all cable connections are secure All tiers
Quarterly Full hardware inspection — open computer cases and clean internal dust; inspect monitor cables for wear; test UPS battery under load; review CCTV footage storage and camera alignment Standard and Premium tiers
Annually UPS battery replacement check — most UPS batteries last 2–3 years under regular use; test generator under full load; review hardware age and plan replacements for units over 5 years old All tiers
Maintenance log: Keep a simple written maintenance log — date, what was done, any issues found. This is invaluable for tracking recurring problems, warranty claims, and for handing over to a new technician or staff member.
